Burger

Description

A burger is a classic comfort food made with a juicy beef patty served inside a soft bun, usually topped with cheese, fresh vegetables, and flavorful sauces. It is simple to prepare and can be customized with a variety of toppings.

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Ground beef
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Form the ground beef into burger patties and season both sides with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and cook the patties for about 4–5 minutes on each side.
  3. Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking and let it melt.
  4. Toast the burger buns lightly in the pan.
  5. Spread ketchup and mayonnaise on the buns.
  6. Place the lettuce, tomato, and onion on the bottom bun.
  7. Add the cooked burger patty and top it with pickles.
  8. Place the top bun over the burger and serve.
